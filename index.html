I am a Lecturer at Information Support Force Engineering University. I received my PhD from the College of Computer, National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), advised by Prof. Liqian Chen and Prof. Ji Wang. My research interests include program analysis and verification, software testing, AI-driven software vulnerability discovery, and analysis and testing of neuro-symbolic software, with a focus on abstract interpretation, loop invariant inference, and combining large language models with formal methods.
